What it is

An unofficial, play-money recreation of the classic three-reel Wheel of Fortune slot machine with the big bonus wheel on top. Plain HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no build, runs from any static host or straight from disk. The reels are weighted virtual-stop steppers exactly like the real machines, so blanks tease near-misses, and the whole par sheet (hit frequency, wheel odds, return) is computed exactly by the bundled tool.

Features

Mechanical steppers

Three 22-stop reels with blanks, weighted stops, stepper-motor whir, mechanical clunks, rising stop tones and a quick spring bounce onto the payline.

The wheel

22 weighted wedges from 25 to 1,000 credits. SPIN on the third reel with three coins lights the cabinet, the crowd chants, and a constant-deceleration spin with a clicking flapper lands exactly on the predetermined wedge.

Coins, credits, jackpot

Bet one, bet max, pull the handle, cash out to the hopper. Three logos on three coins win a progressive jackpot that grows with every bet.

Cabinet optics

Casino-floor backdrop, glossy cabinet with chasing lights, pay glass and bonus rules, LED meters, chrome trims, a lever that actually pulls.

Full sound

All effects synthesized live in Web Audio; the chant is a generated voice layered into a crowd.

Verified odds

node tools/rtp-sim.js prints the exact par sheet: pay frequencies, wheel average, return for 1, 2 and 3 coins.

Run it yourself

Open index.html, or serve the folder (python -m http.server 8766 or start.cmd on Windows). The README covers the configuration, the par-sheet tool and the screenshot script.
